    How it works

    Each charge is calculated as billed − insurance paid − adjustments = estimated patient amount. Compare the estimate to the amount on your explanation of benefits (EOB) and final provider statement. Similar descriptions are flagged for manual review, but they are not proof of duplicate billing.

    Important: ApprovalMatch is an educational organizer, not medical, legal, insurance, or financial advice. It does not verify codes, negotiated rates, benefits, or claim eligibility. Never send patient names, account numbers, or other sensitive medical information into a public repository.
